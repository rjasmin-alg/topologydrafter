Privacy

Last updated 3 October 2026

Short version: what you draw stays in your browser. TopologyDrafter has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts or fonts. Apart from loading the app itself, it makes no requests to this server or anyone else.

What is stored, and where

The app keeps two entries in your browser's local storage, on your own device. Neither is ever sent to the server:

  • topologydrafter.autosave.v2 — the diagram you are working on, so it is still there if you close the tab or reload.
  • topologydrafter.prefs.v2 — your editor settings: light or dark theme, grid, snapping, cable routing and the default link type.

Both exist only to provide the editor you are using, so they need no consent. To remove them, clear this site's data in your browser settings. Private or incognito windows discard them when you close the window.

Files, export and the clipboard

Opening and saving .json files, exporting PNG or SVG, and copying to the clipboard all happen locally in your browser. Files go only where you put them.

Links to other sites

The source link opens the project's page on GitHub, which has its own privacy policy. Nothing is loaded from GitHub unless you click it.
